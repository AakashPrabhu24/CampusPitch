TEST SCENARIO 1: STUDENT FOUNDER VOICE & NATURAL LANGUAGE PITCH
================================================================================
1. On the main Role Gate screen, select the "Student Founder" tab.
2. Sign in or register with any student email (e.g. student@iitb.ac.in / Password123!),
   or tap the pre-filled demo login.
3. You will land on the "Student Founder Studio".

4. Pitch Intake (Voice or Natural Text):
   - Option A (Voice): Tap the large Mic button (allow microphone permission if prompted)
     and speak your pitch naturally.
   - Option B (Text Input): In the bottom text field, copy and paste this sentence:

     "I want to upload an app called MediCare. It manages hospitals and is looking for an investment of 2 crore."

     Then tap the Send (arrow) button.

5. Verify AI Extraction & Confirmation Card:
   - Within 1-2 seconds, the green-bordered card titled "Did I get this right?" appears:
       * App Name: MediCare
       * Description: It manages hospitals
       * Sector: Healthcare / SaaS
       * Stage: MVP
       * Seeking Investment: Rs 2,00,00,000 (correctly formatted from "2 crore")

6. Confirm & Upload:
   - Tap the green button: [✓] Confirm & Upload
   - A success notification confirms: "MediCare published to Central Data Lake!"
   - The card appears under "Your Published MVPs" with real-time sync.

TEST SCENARIO 2: ONE-CLICK GITHUB REPOSITORY IMPORT
================================================================================
1. On the Student Founder Studio, find the buttons below the input card.
2. Tap "Import GitHub".
3. A dedicated dialog window "Import GitHub Repository" opens immediately.
4. Choose either:
   - One-Tap Demo: Tap the [medicare] chip (pre-fills "childchiller/medicare"), OR
   - Custom Repo: Type any public repo (e.g., torvalds/linux or square/retrofit).
5. Tap "Import Now".
6. The green-bordered "Did I get this right?" card appears with the repo name,
   tech stack description, and an initial target raise (Rs 25,00,000).
7. Tap [✓] Confirm & Upload to publish it to Firestore.
